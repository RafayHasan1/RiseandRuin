Privacy policy

Rise and Ruin Uploader. Last updated: November 3, 2026.

Rise and Ruin Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Rise & Ruin YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel and to send approval emails to the owner.

Data accessed

The app uses Google sign-in to access the owner's YouTube account (to upload videos) and Gmail account (to send emails to the owner). It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's own videos and send notification emails. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored securely in the owner's automation software and are not shared with anyone.

Google API Services

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
